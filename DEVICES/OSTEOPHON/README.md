# OSTEOPHON

**Four-Channel Percussive Relic**

**Reactive Relics — Experimental Device**

OSTEOPHON is a four-channel percussive instrument developed within the Reactive Relics instrumentarium.

The device translates physical impact into electronic gate signals through four piezoelectric sensors.

Its physical interface is not a conventional drumstick or electronic trigger. The instrument is played using **bone-like percussion objects fabricated by Fran**, turning the act of striking the relic into a ritualised gesture.

---

## CONCEPT

OSTEOPHON explores the relationship between **bone, impact, vibration and electronic sound**.

The performer strikes the instrument using sculptural bone-like objects. Piezoelectric elements embedded within the percussion tools detect the resulting vibration and transform each impact into an independent electronic gate.

The instrument therefore exists simultaneously as:

* percussion instrument
* sensing system
* sculptural object
* electronic interface
* ritual artefact

---

## THE BONE PERCUSSORS

The percussion tools are custom objects fabricated by Fran.

The piezoelectric sensors can be integrated into the tips of the bone-like structures and protected within a PVC layer or enclosure.

This makes the sensor part of the physical instrument rather than an external component attached to it.

```text
BONE PERCUSSOR
      │
      ▼
   [ PIEZO ]
      │
   PROTECTION
      │
      ▼
    IMPACT
      │
      ▼
   ELECTRONIC
    SIGNAL
```

The physical construction of the percussors is therefore part of the instrument's sensing architecture.

---

## FOUR CHANNELS

OSTEOPHON uses four independent piezoelectric sensing channels.

```text
PIEZO 1 ───► GATE 1
PIEZO 2 ───► GATE 2
PIEZO 3 ───► GATE 3
PIEZO 4 ───► GATE 4
```

Each physical impact can therefore become an independent trigger within the electronic system.

The four channels allow the instrument to behave as a compact electronic percussion interface while retaining the physical character of an experimental relic.

```text

GATE 2
GATE 3

```

These outputs can be used to trigger external synthesizer, percussion or control systems.

## RELIC TAIKO

OSTEOPHON can be understood as a **Relic Taiko**: an electronic percussion instrument inspired by the physical language of ceremonial drumming, but reconstructed through sensing technology and sculptural objects.

The emphasis is not on reproducing a traditional instrument.

It is on creating a new ritual instrument from fragments of familiar physical languages.
