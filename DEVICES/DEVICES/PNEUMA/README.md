# PNEUMA

**THE DEVIL'S JACKPOT**

**Reactive Relics — Experimental Device**

PNEUMA is a breath-activated electronic relic developed within the Reactive Relics instrumentarium.

The device transforms the act of blowing into an offering to an unpredictable machine. Breath becomes signal; signal becomes chance; chance becomes verdict.

Part instrument, part gambling machine and part ritual artefact, PNEUMA explores the relationship between **breath, activation, fortune and symbolic power**.

---

## CONCEPT

PNEUMA is conceived as a **technological lottery of the damned**.

The performer must give something of themselves to activate the relic: their breath.

The machine receives this gesture and produces a response whose meaning is deliberately ambiguous — somewhere between game, divination and ritual.

The interaction is simple:

```text
BREATH
  │
  ▼
ACTIVATION
  │
  ▼
THE MACHINE
  │
  ▼
CHANCE
  │
  ▼
VERDICT
```

The performer does not simply press a button.

They **feed the relic with air** and wait for its answer.

---

## SYMBOLISM

PNEUMA takes inspiration from the visual language of gambling machines, occult symbolism and ritual objects.

Numbers, symbols and combinations can become signs within the device's logic.

The apparent promise of a jackpot is transformed into something more uncertain: a possible reward, a failed invocation, or an unreadable response.

The machine therefore occupies an unstable space between:

* gambling
* divination
* ritual
* technology
* performance

## HARDWARE

### Core system

* Digital microphone / breath sensor
* Microcontroller
* Potentiometer / physical control
* Jack connection
* Electronic audio / signal processing

The breath sensor provides the primary interaction.

The physical controls and connections allow the machine's response to be shaped and integrated into the wider Reactive Relics instrumentarium.

---

## INTERACTION

PNEUMA is activated through breath rather than conventional button input.

```text
PERFORMER
   │
   │ breath
   ▼
MICROPHONE / SENSOR
   │
   ▼
MICROCONTROLLER
   │
   ├── SYMBOLS
   ├── CHANCE
   └── AUDIO / OUTPUT
```

The physical act of blowing becomes the trigger for the machine's internal process.

---

## THE JACKPOT

The jackpot is not simply a reward.

It is part of the fiction of the relic.

The machine presents the possibility of fortune while simultaneously questioning whether the performer is actually controlling the outcome.

The **Devil's Jackpot** is therefore less a conventional game than a ritual proposition:

> Give the machine your breath.
> Accept its answer.
