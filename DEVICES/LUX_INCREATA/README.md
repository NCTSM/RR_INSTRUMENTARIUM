# LUX INCREATA

*The Uncreated Light*

**Reactive Relics — Experimental Device**

LUX INCREATA is an experimental light-based instrument developed within the Reactive Relics instrumentarium.

The device explores light as a material, signal and presence. Electronic processes are translated into luminous behaviour, turning the instrument itself into a visible and responsive artefact.

Its name refers to the theological concept of the **Uncreated Light**: a light understood not as an ordinary physical object, but as a manifestation of the divine.

Within Reactive Relics, this idea becomes a technological fiction.

---

## CONCEPT

LUX INCREATA treats light as something that can be **invoked, shaped and witnessed**.

Rather than using illumination simply to reveal an object, the device makes light itself the visible output of an electronic process.

The instrument therefore occupies a space between:

* light source
* synthesizer
* electronic instrument
* ritual object
* speculative relic

The physical behaviour of the light becomes part of the performance.
